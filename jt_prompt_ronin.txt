This is a RONIN FLANGES AND FITTINGS (Ronin, "rff" logo top left) JOB TRANSFER form, partly handwritten.
Ronin is the sister company of Mining Pressure Systems (MPS): the form records Ronin stock (flanges and fittings)
transferred to an MPS job, to MPS, or to a supplier or customer.
Extract the fields into the JSON schema.
Rules:
- jt_number: the printed red form number next to "DF" at the top right (e.g. DF 16593). Give the digits only (16593).
- Transcribe handwriting exactly as written; do not correct spelling or expand abbreviations.
- Only output line items for rows that actually contain handwriting. Do not output empty rows.
- A new line item starts only where a QUANTITY is written. Writers often leave an empty row above each new
  quantity, so an empty row followed by a quantity = a new item. Text in the following rows without a
  quantity is a continuation: append it to the previous item's description, separated by " | "
  (e.g. "for J/No 33387E" written under an item belongs to that item).
- Exception: a note in the description column that has an empty row above it and NO quantity
  (e.g. "PLEASE RETURN BY FRIDAY", "URGENT") belongs to the whole form, not an item: put it in remarks
  (after anything written in the remarks box, separated by " | ").
- For every line item set blank_row_above: true when the printed table row directly above the item's
  first row has no handwriting (or the item is in the first row), false when that row has handwriting.
  If you are unsure whether text is a continuation or a note, output it as its own line item with
  quantity and item_no null and the correct blank_row_above; it is sorted out afterwards.
- The quantity is a number in the QUANTITY column at the left of the table; look carefully,
  it may be faint or overwritten.
- ITEM NO. is the Ronin stock code, written in the narrow second column. Copy it character by character with no
  spaces. Ronin codes are: 4 digits for the nominal bore (0015 = 15NB, 0080 = 80NB, 0250 = 250NB, 1000 = 1000NB),
  then the class (1000 3 = 1000/3, 1600 3 = 1600/3, 6003 = 600/3, 150A = ASA 150#, 300A = ASA 300#, 600A = ASA 600#,
  63 = BS4504 6/3, 163 = BS4504 16/3), sometimes a schedule (A20, A40, A80 = SCH20/40/80), then the type:
  SOFF (slip-on flat face), SORF (slip-on raised face), RFST (raised-face stepbore), FFST (flat-face stepbore),
  RFWN or WN (weld neck), BL (blank), SCRD (screwed), COMWN (compact weld neck).
  Examples: 008010003SOFF, 0250600A20RFST, 0100150ABL, 010016003SOFF. Code letters O and digits 0 look alike:
  the nominal-bore and class part is digits, the type at the end is letters. If the column is empty: null.
- Ignore diagonal or squiggly lines drawn through empty rows.
- An "ALAC PROPERTIES SECURITY CHECK" rubber stamp is usually at the top right, with a handwritten date and
  initials or a signature inside it: put the date written in the stamp, exactly as written, in receiving_stamp
  (null when there is no stamp or no date in it).
- CUSTOMER is who the stock goes to, often M.P.S. (= MPS).
- TRANSFERRED FROM / TO are companies or places (FROM is usually RONIN). A person's name or signature written by the
  receiver belongs in received_by, not in transferred_to.
- TRANSFERRED BY (bottom left) is usually a written first name and surname in joined-up handwriting: transcribe it
  into transferred_by as well as you can read it. RECEIVED BY (bottom right) is often only a signature.
- Signatures that are scribbles, not legible names: "[signature]". A field with nothing written: null.
- Illegible text: "[illegible]". Never guess.
Terms that commonly appear on these forms:
M.P.S. JOB NO. format: 4 or 5 digits followed by E (e.g. 1075E, 33326E), rarely D,
  or the words CONS (consumables), STOCK or MIN003. The trailing letter is sometimes left off.
DATE may be written day first (26-10-2026, 26/10/26) or year first (2026/10/26). Copy it exactly as written.
